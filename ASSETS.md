The following are derived from official DDLC material:

| Path | What |
| --- | --- |
| `lua/ddlc/palette.lua`, `colors/` | the base16 schemes from [ddlc-palette](https://github.com/rokokol/ddlc-palette), which measures every colour off [ddlc.moe](https://ddlc.moe/). The mapping from slot to highlight group is mine, the values are theirs |
